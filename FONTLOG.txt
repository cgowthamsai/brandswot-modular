FONTLOG for BRANDSWOT Modular
=============================

This file provides detailed information on the BRANDSWOT Modular Font
Software. This information should be distributed along with the
BRANDSWOT Modular fonts and any derivative works.


Basic Font Information
----------------------

BRANDSWOT Modular is a pixel display typeface built from brickwork.
Every pixel of its 5 x 7 grid is filled with three courses of
running-bond masonry, so the letters read as built, not drawn. It was
designed by Gowtham Sai for BRANDSWOT Consultancy, Hyderabad, India,
as the display voice of the brand line "We don't just brand. We build."

The family ships as two variable fonts (Roman and Italic) with a weight
axis from Light 300 to Black 900. Weight is expressed through the
mortar: Light opens the joints, Black closes them.

Character set: Google Fonts Latin Core (Western, Central and Eastern
European Latin), Indian Rupee sign, arrows, combining marks.


ChangeLog
---------

04 Oct 2026 (Gowtham Sai) BRANDSWOT Modular v2.100
- Rebuilt from an editable pixel source (sources/BRANDSWOTModular.pixels).
- Weight axis 300-900 (Light, Regular, Medium, SemiBold, Bold, ExtraBold,
  Black). Bold 700 is identical to the original Display Bold cut.
- Italic family (12 degree slant).
- Proportional spacing: narrow letters (i, l, j, punctuation) no longer
  sit in a full-width cell.
- Google Fonts Latin Core coverage: 210+ new characters, every accented
  letter, combining marks with mark positioning, smart quotes,
  guillemets, ordinals, (c) (R) TM and more.
- j now carries its dot at the same height as i.
- Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

2026 (Gowtham Sai) BRANDSWOT Modular Display Bold v2.000
- First release: single Display Bold cut, ASCII plus currency and arrows.
