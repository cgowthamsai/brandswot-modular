BRANDSWOT Modular is a pixel display typeface built from brickwork. Every pixel of its 5 × 7 grid is laid in three courses of running-bond masonry, so the letters read as built, not drawn.

The family has a weight axis from Light 300 to Black 900 in Roman and Italic. Weight lives in the mortar: Light opens the joints, Black closes them, and the letterforms stay the same. It covers Google Fonts Latin Core, including the Indian Rupee sign, and works best for headlines, signage, packaging and motion titles above 32 px.

Designed by Gowtham Sai for BRANDSWOT Consultancy in Hyderabad, India. To contribute, see github.com/cgowthamsai/brandswot-modular.
